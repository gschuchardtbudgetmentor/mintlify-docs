## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(e) Subawards (Line G.5)**
>
> Except for the purpose of obtaining goods and services for the proposer's own use which creates a procurement relationship with a contractor, **no portion of the proposed activity may be subawarded or transferred to another organization without prior written NSF authorization**.
>
> If known at the time of proposal submission, the intent to enter into such arrangements must be disclosed in the proposal. A **separate budget and a budget justification of no more than five pages** must be provided for each subrecipient, if already identified. The description of the work to be performed must be included in the project description.
>
> All proposing organizations are required to make a case-by-case determination regarding the role of a subrecipient versus contractor for each agreement. 2 CFR § 200.331 provides characteristics of each.
>
> **Indirect Cost Rates for Subrecipients:**
>
> NSF does not negotiate rates for subrecipients. Per 2 CFR § 200.332, NSF recipients must use the **domestic subrecipient's applicable U.S. Federally negotiated indirect cost rate(s)**. If none exists, the appropriate rate will be:
>
> - a negotiated rate between the NSF recipient and the subrecipient;
> - a prior rate negotiated between a different pass-through entity and the same subrecipient; OR
> - the **de minimis rate of 10% of MTDC**.
>
> For **foreign subrecipients**, NSF expects recipients use the foreign sub's applicable U.S. Federally negotiated rate if available. If not, the foreign subrecipient is limited to the **de minimis 10% of MTDC**.
>
> Proposers are responsible for ensuring that proposed subrecipient costs, including indirect costs, are reasonable and appropriate.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (e) Subawards

<Tip>
  Plain English: A **subaward** is when another institution carries out a defined chunk of your scientific work — their PI, their team, their resources. You "subaward" them through your NSF grant. This goes on Line G.5.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  Subawards **must be disclosed at proposal submission**. Adding a subaward post-award requires NSF Grants Officer prior approval. Don't try to slip one in later — get them in the proposal.
</Danger>

#### Each subaward needs

<Check>
  - **Separate budget** (full A-M breakdown)
  - **Separate budget justification — max 5 pages**
  - Work description in the project description
  - Letter of intent / authorized signature from the sub institution
  - Statement of work
</Check>

#### Subaward vs Contractor — the call

<Warning>
  - **Subaward** = collaborator carrying out a portion of the funded scientific work; they're a partner, not a vendor
  - **Contractor** = vendor providing goods or services for your benefit, available to the general market (e.g., sequencing services, manufacturing)

  Use 2 CFR § 200.331 characteristics to decide. Contractors go on G.6, not G.5.
</Warning>

#### Indirect Costs on Subawards (this part trips everyone up)

<Tip>
  Plain English: NSF doesn't negotiate F&A rates for subrecipients. Here's the cascade for domestic subs:

  1. **Use their federally-negotiated rate** if they have one (most universities do)
  2. If they don't, use a rate previously negotiated by another pass-through with them
  3. Otherwise, use **10% de minimis of MTDC**
</Tip>

<Warning>
  For **foreign subrecipients**: same cascade, but if no federally-negotiated rate exists, the foreign sub is capped at **10% de minimis MTDC**. They CAN'T propose a higher rate.
</Warning>

#### MTDC base on subawards (the \$25K rule)

<Danger>
  **Critical**: For YOUR own indirect cost calculation, you can only charge indirect on the **first \$25,000** of each subaward total cost. After \$25K, the subaward amount is excluded from your MTDC base. The subrecipient charges their full indirect rate on their own budget — but you only get F&A on the first \$25K of theirs in your MTDC.
</Danger>

#### Foreign Subawards

<Check>
  See also Chapter I.E.2 — additional NSF requirements apply when issuing a subaward or consultant arrangement to a foreign organization or foreign individual. You'll need to justify in the project description WHY a foreign sub is necessary.
</Check>
