> ### **(e) Prohibition on Certain Telecommunications and Video Surveillance Services or Equipment**
>
> Section 889 of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2019 (Public Law 115-232) prohibits the head of an executive agency from obligating or expending loan or grant funds to procure or obtain, extend, or renew a contract to procure or obtain, or enter into a contract (or extend or renew a contract) to procure or obtain the equipment, services, or systems as identified in section 889 of the NDAA for FY 2019. See 2 CFR §§ 200.216, 200.471, Chapter X.F, and the applicable award terms and conditions for additional information.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (e) Prohibition on Certain Telecom and Video Surveillance Equipment

<Danger>
  **No NSF funds for "covered" telecom/surveillance equipment** from specific Chinese companies (Huawei, ZTE, Hytera, Hikvision, Dahua) or their subsidiaries — per NDAA Section 889.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: You can't use NSF money to buy phones, cameras, network gear, or services from these flagged companies — even indirectly through a vendor.
</Tip>

<Check>
  Covered entities (the "Section 889 list"):

  - Huawei Technologies Company
  - ZTE Corporation
  - Hytera Communications Corporation
  - Hangzhou Hikvision Digital Technology Company
  - Dahua Technology Company
  - Any subsidiaries or affiliates of the above
</Check>

<Warning>
  The prohibition extends to **systems that use** covered equipment as a substantial or essential component — not just direct purchases.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Your organization should have a Section 889 representation in SAM. Talk to your sponsored programs office if you're uncertain whether a planned purchase is affected.
</Note>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (e) Prohibition on Certain Telecommunications and Video Surveillance Equipment

<Danger>
  Per Section 889 of the FY2019 NDAA, NSF (and you, as a recipient) **cannot use grant funds** to procure, obtain, extend, or renew contracts for covered telecom or video surveillance equipment/services.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: This is the "Huawei / ZTE / Hikvision / Dahua / Hytera" rule. NSF cannot fund — and you cannot buy with NSF money — covered equipment or services from these and other listed Chinese telecom/surveillance providers.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  This applies to direct purchases AND to extending/renewing existing contracts. Check before renewing any institutional contract with grant funds.
</Warning>

<Check>
  Background references:

  - 2 CFR § 200.216
  - 2 CFR § 200.471
  - PAPPG Chapter X.F
  - Applicable award terms and conditions
</Check>

<Note>
  Your sponsored programs office should already have procedures for Section 889 compliance — coordinate with them before procuring telecom or surveillance gear on a grant.
</Note>

> ### **(e) Prohibition on Certain Telecommunications and Video Surveillance Services or Equipment**
>
> In accordance with 2 CFR § 200.216, recipients and subrecipients are prohibited from obligating or expending loan or grant funds to: (1) Procure or obtain; (2) Extend or renew a contract to procure or obtain; or (3) Enter into a contract (or extend or renew a contract) to procure or obtain equipment, services, or systems that uses covered telecommunications equipment or services as a substantial or essential component of any system, or as critical technology as part of any system. As described in Public Law 115-232, section 889, covered telecommunications equipment is telecommunications equipment produced by Huawei Technologies Company or ZTE Corporation (or any subsidiary or affiliate of such entities). Refer to 2 CFR § 200.216 and 2 CFR § 200.471 for additional information.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (e) Prohibition on Certain Telecommunications and Video Surveillance Services or Equipment

<Danger>
  **Unallowable.** You cannot use NSF funds to buy, lease, or extend contracts for equipment, services, or systems that use **covered telecommunications equipment** as a substantial/essential component or critical technology.
</Danger>

<Warning>
  "Covered telecommunications equipment" includes:

  - Huawei Technologies Company
  - ZTE Corporation
  - Hytera Communications
  - Hangzhou Hikvision Digital Technology
  - Dahua Technology
  - Any subsidiary or affiliate of these entities
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Plain English: This came from Section 889 of the 2019 NDAA (Public Law 115-232). Common gotchas: networking gear, smartphones, video surveillance/CCTV cameras, badge readers — check the manufacturer before purchasing with grant funds.
</Tip>

<Check>
  This applies to subrecipients and contractors too. Flow the requirement down in your subaward and contract terms.
</Check>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (e) Prohibition on Certain Telecommunications and Video Surveillance Services or Equipment

<Tip>
  Plain English: Section 889 of the 2019 NDAA bans federal grant money from being used to buy or use telecom/video surveillance equipment or services from certain Chinese companies (Huawei, ZTE, Hytera, Hikvision, Dahua, and their subsidiaries/affiliates).
</Tip>

<Danger>
  Do not budget for — or use — covered equipment/services on NSF awards. This is checked at the institutional level and violations can trigger termination and other consequences.
</Danger>

<Warning>
  Even using covered equipment somewhere else in your operation (not on NSF money directly) can disqualify your organization from receiving NSF funding. Check with your IT and compliance offices.
</Warning>

<Note>
  See 2 CFR §§ 200.216, 200.471 and the PAPPG Chapter X.F for the detailed rules and exceptions.
</Note>
