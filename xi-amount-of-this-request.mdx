## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(xi) Amount of This Request (Line L on the Proposal Budget)**
>
> The total amount of funds requested by the proposer.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (xi) Amount of This Request

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line L = the total NSF funds you're asking for. For most proposals this equals Line J. For SBIR/STTR/Major Facilities with fees, Line L = Line J \+ Line K.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Line L = Line J \+ Line K (Fees)**

  Where for most proposals, Line K = 0, so **Line L = Line J**.
</Check>

<Note>
  Line L is the "bottom-line" number reviewers and Program Officers see when assessing your total funding request.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Make sure the Line L total in Year 1, Year 2, etc. plus the cumulative total matches what you describe narratively in the budget justification and project description. Inconsistencies between budget and narrative are a top reason proposals come back with questions.
</Warning>
