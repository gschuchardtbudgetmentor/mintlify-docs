## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(d) Confidential Budgetary Information**
>
> The proposing organization may request that salary data on senior/key personnel not be released to persons outside the Government during the review process. In such cases, the item for senior/key personnel salaries in the proposal may appear as a single figure and the person-months represented by that amount omitted.
>
> If this option is exercised, senior/key personnel salaries and person-months must be itemized in a separate statement and forwarded to NSF in accordance with the instructions specified in Chapter II.E.1. This statement must include all of the information requested on the proposal budget for each person involved.
>
> NSF will not forward the detailed information to reviewers and will hold it privileged to the extent permitted by law. The information on senior/key personnel salaries will be used as the basis for determining the salary amounts shown in the budget.
>
> The box for **"Proprietary or Privileged Information"** must be checked on the Cover Sheet when the proposal contains confidential budgetary information.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (d) Confidential Budgetary Information

<Tip>
  Plain English: If you don't want reviewers (external researchers) to see how much your senior/key personnel get paid, you can lump their salaries into a single figure on the budget and hide the person-months — then send NSF a separate, sealed document with the detailed breakdown.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  This is **optional** and rarely used. Most institutions just disclose salary detail normally. Use this only if there's a genuine confidentiality reason (e.g., competitive industry, privacy concerns).
</Warning>

#### How to do it

<Check>
  1. On the proposal budget: enter a **single combined salary figure** for senior/key personnel and **leave person-months blank**.
  2. Send NSF a **separate itemized statement** with full salary and person-months detail per person.
  3. Submit the separate statement following Chapter II.E.1 (Proprietary Information).
  4. **Check the "Proprietary or Privileged Information" box** on the Cover Sheet.
</Check>

<Danger>
  If you forget to check the "Proprietary or Privileged Information" box on the Cover Sheet, NSF may treat your confidential statement as regular proposal content. Always check the box.
</Danger>

<Note>
  NSF will use the confidential detail to set the actual award salary amounts but won't share it with reviewers, and will hold it privileged to the extent the law allows.
</Note>
