## Information Key

## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

> ### **f. Submission of Proposals by Former NSF Staff**
>
> For one year following separation from the Foundation, any communication with NSF by a former employee or IPA must be done through use of a "substitute negotiator." Unless a substitute negotiator has been designated by the proposer/recipient, the Division of Grants and Agreements (DGA) or the Division of Acquisition and Cooperative Support (DACS) will not process a new proposal with a former employee or IPA as PI or co-PI.
>
> If it has been less than a year since a former employee separated from NSF or an IPA ended their appointment and they submit a proposal, documentation from the AOR needs to be included which designates a substitute negotiator for that proposal. The substitute negotiator must be from the same organization as the PI or co-PI for whom the negotiator is required. A co-PI on a new proposal should designate the PI as the substitute negotiator. This information should be submitted as a single copy document and uploaded in the "Additional Single Copy Documents" category.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### f. Former NSF Staff Submissions

<Tip>
  Plain English: If you (PI or co-PI) worked at NSF as a federal employee or IPA detailee within the **past 12 months**, you can't directly communicate with NSF about a new proposal. Your institution must designate a **substitute negotiator** to talk to NSF on your behalf.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  Without a designated substitute negotiator, NSF's Grants and Agreements office will **not process** your proposal. Period.
</Danger>

#### How to designate a substitute negotiator

<Check>
  - The substitute negotiator must be at the **same organization** as the affected PI/co-PI
  - For a former-NSF **co-PI**: the PI on the proposal can serve as substitute negotiator
  - The designation comes from your **AOR**
  - Submit the designation as a **Single Copy Document** under "Additional Single Copy Documents"
</Check>

<Warning>
  The 12-month restriction starts from your separation date (end of federal appointment for employees, end of IPA appointment for rotators).
</Warning>

<Note>
  This is part of NSF's broader ethics framework to avoid the appearance of former staff using insider relationships to influence award decisions.
</Note>
