## Information Key

## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

> ### **e. Proposal Certifications for Senior/Key Personnel**
>
> **(i) William M. (Mac) Thornberry NDAA FY2021, Section 223(a)(1) (42 U.S.C. § 6605(a)(1))**
>
> Each individual identified as a senior/key person is required to certify in SciENcv that the information provided in the Biographical Sketch and Current and Pending (Other) Support documents are accurate, current, and complete.
>
> Senior/key personnel are required to update their Current and Pending (Other) Support disclosures prior to award, and at any subsequent time the agency determines appropriate during the term of the award.
>
> **(ii) Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Programs**
>
> Pursuant to Section 10632 (42 U.S.C. § 19232), each individual identified as senior/key person must certify prior to proposal submission that they are not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program and annually thereafter for the duration of the award.
>
> False representations regarding either of the above certifications may be subject to prosecution and liability pursuant to, but not limited to, 18 U.S.C. §§ 287, 1001, 1031 and 31 U.S.C. §§ 3729-3733 and 3802.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### e. Certifications for Senior/Key Personnel

<Tip>
  Plain English: In addition to the organization's certifications, **each individual senior/key person** must personally sign two certifications before the proposal goes out the door. These happen inside SciENcv.
</Tip>

#### (i) Biographical Sketch & C&P Accuracy

<Check>
  Every senior/key person certifies that:

  - Their Biographical Sketch is accurate, current, and complete
  - Their Current and Pending (Other) Support is accurate, current, and complete
</Check>

<Warning>
  This isn't a one-time thing. C&P must be updated **prior to award** and any time NSF requests during the award.
</Warning>

#### (ii) Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Programs

<Danger>
  Each senior/key person certifies they are **not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program** — before submission, and **annually** for the duration of the award.
</Danger>

#### Legal exposure

<Danger>
  False certifications expose individuals to:

  - **18 U.S.C. §§ 287, 1001, 1031** — false statements and false claims (criminal)
  - **31 U.S.C. §§ 3729–3733, 3802** — civil False Claims Act liability

  Recent NSF cases have included criminal prosecution and prison time.
</Danger>

<Note>
  These certifications are completed by each individual **directly in SciENcv** — the AOR cannot certify on their behalf for (i) and (ii) above.
</Note>
