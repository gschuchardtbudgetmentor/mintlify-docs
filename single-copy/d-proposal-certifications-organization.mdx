> ### **d. Proposal Certifications Provided by the Organization**
>
> Government-wide certifications and representations are provided by the proposer on an annual basis in SAM. Note that the box for "Disclosure of Lobbying Activities" must be checked on the Cover Sheet if, pursuant to the Lobbying certification provided in SAM, submission of the SF LLL is required. The AOR must use the "Authorized Organizational Representative function" to sign and submit the proposal, including NSF-specific proposal certifications.
>
> The required NSF-specific proposal certifications are:
>
> **(i) Certification for AOR or Individual Proposer** — AOR certifies accuracy/completeness of the proposal and agreement to comply with award terms.
>
> **(ii) Certification Regarding Conflict of Interest** — Organization has implemented and enforces a written COI policy consistent with Chapter IX.A; all required financial disclosures were made; conflicts are/will be managed before any funds are spent.
>
> **(iii) Certification Regarding Flood Hazard Insurance** — For construction in FEMA-designated flood hazard areas, AORs certify adequate flood insurance has been or will be obtained when: (1) any NSF award for building/facility construction, or (2) any award \>\$25,000 budgeted for building repair/alteration/improvement.
>
> **(iv) Certification Regarding Responsible and Ethical Conduct of Research (RECR)** — Institution has a plan for RECR training (including mentor training) for undergrads, grad students, postdocs, faculty, and other senior/key personnel supported by NSF.
>
> **(v) Certification Regarding Organizational Support** — Per Section 526 of the America COMPETES Reauthorization Act of 2010, organizational support is available for both intellectual merit and broader impacts components.
>
> **(vi) Certification Regarding Dual Use Research of Concern** — Compliance with U.S. Government Policy for Institutional Oversight of Life Sciences DURC.
>
> **(vii) Certification per William M. (Mac) Thornberry NDAA FY2021 Section 223(a)(1)** — Each senior/key person has been made aware of certification requirements under 42 U.S.C. § 6605(a)(1).
>
> **(viii) Safe and Inclusive Working Environments for Off-Campus or Off-Site Research** — For proposals doing off-campus/off-site research, the organization has a plan in place for safe and inclusive working environments. See Chapter II.E.9.
>
> **(ix) Certification Regarding Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Programs** — Per Section 10632 of CHIPS and Science Act of 2022 (42 U.S.C. § 19232), AOR certifies all senior/key personnel have been made aware of and complied with the requirement to certify they are not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### d. Proposal Certifications by the Organization

<Tip>
  Plain English: When your AOR clicks "submit," they're signing a stack of certifications on behalf of the whole organization. These cover everything from "this proposal is accurate" to "we have a COI policy" to NSPM-33 / CHIPS Act compliance.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  The AOR is **personally** certifying these on the organization's behalf. False certification = federal liability for the AOR and the institution.
</Warning>

#### Most-missed certifications

<Danger>
  Pay extra attention to:

  - **(iii) Flood Hazard Insurance** — even "minor" building repairs over \$25K trigger this in FEMA flood zones
  - **(viii) Safe and Inclusive Plan** — required for ANY off-campus/off-site research (fieldwork, vessels, aircraft)
  - **(ix) Malign Foreign Talent** — every senior/key person must be individually informed and have signed their personal certification before AOR can certify on their behalf
</Danger>

#### Cover Sheet checkboxes that interact

<Check>
  - If lobbying certification in SAM triggers SF-LLL → check "Disclosure of Lobbying Activities"
  - If proposal contains proprietary info → check "Proprietary or Privileged Information"
  - If research is off-campus/off-site → check "Off-Campus or Off-Site Research" (triggers Safe and Inclusive plan)
</Check>

<Note>
  Annual SAM registration must be current for these certifications to be valid. Lapsed SAM = blocked submission.
</Note>
