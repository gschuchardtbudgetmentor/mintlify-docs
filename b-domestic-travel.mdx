## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(b) Domestic Travel**
>
> Domestic travel includes travel within and between the U.S., its territories, and possessions.
>
> **Travel, meal, and hotel expenses of recipient employees who are not on travel status are unallowable.**
>
> Costs of employees on travel status are limited to those specifically authorized by 2 CFR § 200.475.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Domestic Travel

<Tip>
  Plain English: Domestic = anywhere in the U.S., U.S. territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands), and possessions.
</Tip>

<Check>
  Common domestic destinations covered:

  - All 50 U.S. states \+ D.C.
  - Puerto Rico
  - U.S. Virgin Islands
  - Guam
  - American Samoa
  - Northern Mariana Islands
</Check>

<Danger>
  **"Not on travel status" = unallowable.** If an employee is at their home institution / regular work location, you cannot charge meals, hotels, or local "travel" for them to the grant — even if the meeting is project-related. This includes local lab meetings, on-campus workshops, etc.
</Danger>

<Warning>
  "Travel status" is a defined term: usually means traveling away from your designated work location for project purposes, generally requiring overnight stay or a substantial distance from your duty station. Check 2 CFR § 200.475 and your institution's travel policy.
</Warning>

#### What's allowable

<Tip>
  Plain English: For employees actually on travel status, you can budget:

  - Round-trip economy airfare (Fly America required)
  - Lodging at locality rate
  - Per diem (meals & incidentals)
  - Ground transportation
  - Conference registration fees
  - Mileage (at federal rate) for personal vehicle use
</Tip>

<Note>
  Most universities use **GSA per diem rates** for U.S. travel. Check the GSA website for the destination city's lodging cap and M&IE rate.
</Note>

#### Common Pitfalls

<Warning>
  - Charging meals for on-campus workshops → unallowable
  - Charging a hotel night for a local-day-trip meeting → unallowable
  - Combining personal vacation with project travel → only the project-related portion is allowable; document carefully
  - Business-class airfare → unallowable absent a documented exception
</Warning>

<Check>
  Reference: 2 CFR § 200.475 (Travel costs).
</Check>
