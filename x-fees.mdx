> ### **(x) Fees (Line K on the Proposal Budget)**
>
> This line is available for use only by the **SBIR/STTR programs** and **Major Facilities programs** when specified in the solicitation.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (x) Fees

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line K (Fees) is a profit/fee margin. Almost no NSF proposal uses it. It's reserved for SBIR/STTR (small business innovation) and certain Major Facilities programs where the solicitation explicitly allows fees.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  If you're not submitting to SBIR, STTR, or a Major Facilities solicitation that explicitly allows fees — **leave Line K at \$0**. Putting a value there for any other proposal type is non-compliant.
</Danger>

<Check>
  **Where Line K applies:**

  - SBIR Phase I and Phase II
  - STTR Phase I and Phase II
  - Major Facilities programs (only if the specific solicitation says so)
</Check>

<Note>
  SBIR/STTR typically allow up to a 7% fee on the total of direct \+ indirect costs. Check your solicitation for the specific allowed fee structure.
</Note>
