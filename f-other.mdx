## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(f) Other (Line G.6)**
>
> Any other direct costs not specified in lines G.1 through G.5 must be identified on line G.6. Such costs must be itemized and detailed in the budget justification.
>
> Examples include:
>
> - **Contracts** for the purpose of obtaining goods and services for the proposer's own use (see 2 CFR § 200.331 for additional information); and
> - **Incentive payments** (e.g., payments to human subjects or incentives to promote completion of a survey). Indirect costs should be calculated on incentive payments in accordance with the organization's approved U.S. Federally negotiated indirect cost rate(s).
>
> Performance based incentive payments to employees as described in 2 CFR § 200.430(f) should not be included in this section of the budget.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (f) Other Direct Costs

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line G.6 is the catch-all for direct costs that don't fit G.1-G.5. Vendor contracts, human subject payments, aircraft rentals, space rentals at off-site facilities, minor building alterations, service charges, etc.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Common G.6 items:**

  - **Contracts** for goods/services (vendor relationships, not subawards)
  - **Human subject incentive payments** (gift cards, cash, survey incentives)
  - Aircraft rental
  - Vessel time
  - Space rental at off-site research facilities
  - Minor building alterations
  - Conference venue rental, catering, event supplies (for NSF-sponsored conferences)
  - Service charges (shipping, equipment maintenance contracts)
  - Reference books/periodicals specifically allocable to the project
</Check>

<Warning>
  Each G.6 item must be **individually itemized and justified**. "Misc. \$15,000" is a guaranteed return-without-review.
</Warning>

#### Human Subject Payments

<Tip>
  Plain English: Payments to research subjects (\$50 for a survey, \$200 for an MRI scan, etc.) go on G.6 — NOT Line F Participant Support. F is for trainees/conference participants; G.6 is for research subjects.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  Indirect costs ARE charged on human subject payments at your institution's normal F&A rate. Don't confuse this with Line F (which is typically F&A-exempt).
</Danger>

#### Employee Performance Incentives — NOT here

<Warning>
  Performance-based bonuses to your own employees (2 CFR § 200.430(f)) belong in salaries/wages (Lines A/B), not G.6.
</Warning>

#### Vendor Contracts vs Subawards

<Note>
  If you're buying a service from a vendor (sequencing, fabrication, manufacturing) — it's a **contract** and goes here on G.6. If another institution is doing a piece of the scientific work — it's a **subaward** and goes on G.5. See 2 CFR § 200.331.
</Note>

<Check>
  G.6 is included in the **MTDC base** — it generates indirect costs.
</Check>
