> ### **(b) Administrative and Clerical Salaries & Wages Policy**
>
> In accordance with 2 CFR § 200.413, the salaries of administrative and clerical staff should normally be treated as indirect costs (F&A). Direct charging of these costs may be appropriate only if all the conditions identified below are met:
>
> (i) Administrative or clerical services are integral to a project or activity; (ii) Individuals involved can be specifically identified with the project or activity; (iii) Such costs are explicitly included in the approved budget or have the prior written approval of the cognizant NSF Grants and Agreements Officer; and (iv) The costs are not also recovered as indirect costs.
>
> Conditions (i), (ii) and (iv) above are particularly relevant for consideration at the budget preparation stage.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Administrative and Clerical Salaries Policy

<Tip>
  Plain English: You normally **cannot** charge admin/clerical staff (secretaries, project coordinators, office managers) directly to an NSF grant. Their costs are baked into your indirect cost rate.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  Direct-charging admin/clerical is the **default-no rule**. You can only do it if you meet **all 4** of these conditions:

  1. The work is **integral** to the project (not generic department admin)
  2. You can specifically identify the person and time on this project
  3. The cost is in the approved NSF budget OR has Grants Officer prior approval
  4. The cost is NOT also recovered through indirect costs (no double-dipping)
</Danger>

<Warning>
  The most common audit finding NSF identifies is direct-charged admin support that didn't meet all 4 tests. If you include any admin/clerical salary, **explain the integrality** explicitly in the budget justification.
</Warning>

<Check>
  "Integral" means the project literally can't be done without that admin work — large center grants, conferences, big training programs, projects requiring extensive participant management. Routine departmental admin doesn't qualify.
</Check>

<Note>
  Even if all 4 conditions are met, you still need it in the approved budget — adding admin/clerical mid-award requires written prior approval from the NSF Grants Officer.
</Note>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Administrative and Clerical Salaries & Wages Policy

<Tip>
  Plain English: By default, your institution's admin and clerical staff (department secretaries, grant managers, etc.) are paid out of your **indirect cost (F&A) rate**, not directly from your grant budget.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  You can only directly charge admin/clerical salaries to the grant if **all four** conditions are met:

  1. Their work is **integral** to the project (not general support)
  2. You can specifically identify them with the project
  3. The costs are explicitly in the approved budget OR have written prior approval from NSF
  4. The costs are **not also recovered** through your indirect cost rate (no double-dipping)
</Danger>

<Warning>
  Conditions 1, 2, and 4 are scrutinized at budget time. If you can't pass all four, the costs belong in indirect, not direct.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Plain English: Example of acceptable direct charging: a dedicated project coordinator for a large multi-PI center, who spends 80% effort coordinating across sites. Example of unacceptable: your department's general administrative assistant.
</Tip>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Administrative and Clerical Salaries & Wages Policy

In accordance with 2 CFR § 200.413, the salaries of administrative and clerical staff should normally be treated as indirect costs (F&A).

<Tip>
  Plain English: Department admins, secretaries, and general clerical staff are usually paid out of indirect costs (F&A). You normally **cannot** put them as direct charges on an NSF budget.
</Tip>

Direct charging of these costs may be appropriate only if **all** the conditions identified below are met:

<Danger>
  All four conditions must be met before you can direct-charge admin/clerical:

  1. **Integral** — Their services are integral to the project (not just helpful)
  2. **Specifically identified** — You can point to specific individuals doing specific project work
  3. **Explicitly in approved budget** — Already on the approved budget or pre-approved in writing by NSF
  4. **No double-dipping** — These costs are NOT also recovered in your indirect rate
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: A common allowable case: a project coordinator on a giant multi-institution Center grant whose entire job is managing the project. A common **not** allowable case: budgeting a piece of your department admin's time because they file your reimbursements.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Conditions (i), (ii), and (iv) need to be addressed up front, **at budget preparation time** — not later. Document the justification clearly in the budget justification.
</Warning>
