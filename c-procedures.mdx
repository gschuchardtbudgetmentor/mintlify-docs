## Information Key

## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

> ### **(c) Procedures**
>
> The names of the PI(s), faculty, and other senior/key personnel and the estimated number of full-time-equivalent person-months for which NSF funding is requested, and the total amount of salaries requested per year, must be listed. For consistency with the NSF cost sharing policy, if person months will be requested for senior/key personnel, a corresponding salary amount must be entered on the budget. If salary and person months are not being requested for an individual designated as a senior/key person, they should be removed from Section A of the budget. Their name(s) will remain on the Cover Sheet and the individual(s) role on the project should be described in the Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources section of the proposal.
>
> For postdoctoral associates and other professionals, the total number of persons for each position must be listed, with the number of full-time-equivalent person-months and total amount of salaries requested per year. For graduate and undergraduate students, secretarial, clerical, technical, etc., whose time will be charged directly to the project, only the total number of persons and total amount of salaries requested per year in each category is required. Compensation classified as salary payments must be requested in the salaries and wages category. Salaries requested must be consistent with the organization's regular practices.
>
> The budget justification should detail the rates of pay by individual for each senior/key person, postdoctoral associates, and other professionals.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (c) Procedures for Salaries

<Tip>
  Plain English: How to actually fill out the salaries section of the budget — who needs to be named, who can be grouped, and what the budget justification has to spell out.
</Tip>

#### Senior/Key Personnel (Line A)

<Check>
  For each PI, Co-PI, and senior/key person, list:

  - Name
  - Person-months (FTE) requested per year
  - Total salary requested per year
</Check>

<Danger>
  **Person-months ↔ salary must match.** If you list person-months, you must list a matching salary. If you list salary, you must list matching person-months. Listing person-months without salary triggers NSF's cost sharing prohibition.
</Danger>

<Warning>
  If a senior/key person is contributing time but you're **not** requesting any of their salary or person-months from NSF: **remove them from Section A.** Their name stays on the Cover Sheet, and you describe their role in the Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources section instead.
</Warning>

#### Postdocs and Other Professionals (Lines A continued)

<Tip>
  Plain English: For postdocs and other professionals, list the number of people in each position, total person-months, and total annual salary. Individual names aren't required, but rates of pay need to be detailed in the budget justification.
</Tip>

#### Grad students, undergrads, technicians, clerical (Line B)

<Note>
  For these categories, just list **number of persons** and **total annual salary** per category. No need to break out person-months per individual.
</Note>

#### Budget justification details

<Check>
  In the budget justification, spell out:

  - Rate of pay by **individual** for each senior/key person
  - Rate of pay for each postdoctoral associate
  - Rate of pay for each "other professional"
</Check>

<Warning>
  Salaries must be consistent with your **organization's regular pay practices.** Inflated rates or off-cycle pay raises just for the NSF budget will get flagged.
</Warning>

### (c) Procedures

<Tip>
  Plain English: This section tells you **how** to actually fill in salaries on the NSF budget — by name, role, person-months, and dollar amounts.

#### Senior/Key Personnel (Section A of the budget)

<Check>
  For each PI, co-PI, and senior/key person, list:

  - Name
  - Estimated FTE person-months requested
  - Total salary requested per year
</Check>

<Danger>
  If you list **person-months for a senior/key person, you MUST also list a corresponding salary amount.** Person-months without salary = cost sharing, which is prohibited unless mandatory per the solicitation.
</Danger>

<Warning>
  If a person is designated as senior/key but you're not requesting any of their salary or person-months, **remove them from Section A entirely**. They stay on the Cover Sheet, and you describe their role in the Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources section.
</Warning>

#### Postdocs and Other Professionals

<Tip>
  Plain English: For postdocs and other professionals, list **total number of people** in each category, total person-months, and total annual salary. Names not required, but rates of pay per individual go in the budget justification.
</Tip>

#### Grad/Undergrad Students, Secretarial, Clerical, Technical

<Tip>
  Plain English: For students and support staff being charged directly to the grant: **count and total dollars only**. No need to list individual person-months.
</Tip>

#### What goes in the budget justification

<Check>
  The budget justification must include **rates of pay by individual** for:

  - Each senior/key person
  - Postdoctoral associates
  - Other professionals
</Check>

<Note>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (c) Procedures

<Tip>
  Plain English: How to actually fill out the salary/wage lines on the proposal budget.
</Tip>

#### Senior/Key Personnel (Line A)

<Danger>
  If you list person-months for a senior/key person, you **must** also list a corresponding salary. Listing person-months with zero salary counts as undisclosed cost sharing — which is prohibited.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: If a senior/key person is contributing time but not getting paid from NSF (e.g., their effort is covered by another source):

  - Remove them from Section A of the budget
  - Keep their name on the Cover Sheet
  - Describe their role in **Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources** instead
</Tip>

#### Postdocs and Other Professionals (Line B)

<Tip>
  Plain English: List total **number of persons** in each position, **person-months**, and **total salary**. The budget justification breaks out individual rates.
</Tip>

#### Graduate Students, Undergrads, Secretarial, Technical (Line B)

<Tip>
  Plain English: For these categories, you only need the **total number of persons** and **total salary** per year. No need to break out person-months by individual.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Salaries must match your institution's regular pay practices. Don't inflate rates to fit a budget — NSF will compare against your institutional norms.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Compensation goes in the salaries and wages line. Stipends and trainee allowances for participants go in **Participant Support** (Line F), not here.
</Note>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (c) Procedures

The names of the PI(s), faculty, and other senior/key personnel and the estimated number of full-time-equivalent person-months for which NSF funding is requested, and the total amount of salaries requested per year, must be listed.

<Tip>
  Plain English: For senior/key personnel — list each one **by name**, with person-months and dollar salary they'll get from this NSF grant, every year of the budget.
</Tip>

For consistency with the NSF cost sharing policy, if person months will be requested for senior/key personnel, a corresponding salary amount must be entered on the budget.

<Danger>
  Person-months and salary go together. You **cannot** list person-months without an associated salary number — that would count as unauthorized voluntary committed cost sharing.
</Danger>

If salary and person months are not being requested for an individual designated as a senior/key person, they should be removed from Section A of the budget. Their name(s) will remain on the Cover Sheet and the individual(s) role on the project should be described in the Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources section of the proposal.

<Tip>
  Plain English: If a senior/key person is contributing intellectually but **not** drawing salary, take them off Line A entirely. They stay on the Cover Sheet, and you describe their contribution in Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources.
</Tip>

For postdoctoral associates and other professionals, the total number of persons for each position must be listed, with the number of full-time-equivalent person-months and total amount of salaries requested per year.

<Check>
  Postdocs and other professionals: number of people \+ person-months \+ total annual salary.
</Check>

For graduate and undergraduate students, secretarial, clerical, technical, etc., whose time will be charged directly to the project, only the total number of persons and total amount of salaries requested per year in each category is required.

<Note>
  Grad students, undergrads, and clerical/technical staff: just count \+ total dollars. No person-months needed by individual.
</Note>

The budget justification should detail the rates of pay by individual for each senior/key person, postdoctoral associates, and other professionals.

<Warning>
  Budget justification must break out individual rates of pay for senior/key personnel, postdocs, and other professionals. Salaries must match your institution's standard practices — you can't pay anyone above their normal rate just because NSF is the source.
</Warning>
