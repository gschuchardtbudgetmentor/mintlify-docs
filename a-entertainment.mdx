## Information Key

## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

> ### **(a) Entertainment**
>
> Costs of entertainment, amusement, diversion and social activities, and any costs directly associated with such activities (such as tickets to shows or sporting events, meals, lodging, rentals, transportation, and gratuities) are unallowable. When costs typically considered as entertainment are necessary to accomplish the proposed objectives, they must be included in the budget and justified in the budget justification. Travel, meal, and hotel expenses of recipient employees who are not on travel status are unallowable. See also 2 CFR § 200.438.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (a) Entertainment

<Danger>
  **Entertainment costs are unallowable** — including show tickets, sporting events, social outings, and any associated meals, lodging, transport, or gratuities.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: No team-building outings, no museum trips for visiting collaborators, no celebration dinners.
</Tip>

#### Narrow exception

<Warning>
  If an "entertainment" cost is **genuinely necessary** to accomplish project objectives (e.g., observing audience behavior at a performance for a research study), it can be budgeted — but you must justify it explicitly in the budget justification.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Local employee meals/hotels (not on travel status) are always unallowable per 2 CFR § 200.438.
</Note>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (a) Entertainment

<Danger>
  Entertainment is **unallowable**. This includes:

  - Show tickets, sporting event tickets
  - Meals associated with entertainment
  - Lodging, rentals, transportation for entertainment
  - Gratuities for entertainment
</Danger>

<Warning>
  Narrow exception: If an "entertainment-like" cost is genuinely necessary to accomplish proposal objectives (e.g., a cultural event you're studying as part of an anthropology project), include it in the budget AND justify it explicitly in the budget justification.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Travel, meal, and hotel expenses of employees who are NOT on travel status are unallowable. You can't take the lab to dinner and charge NSF.
</Note>
