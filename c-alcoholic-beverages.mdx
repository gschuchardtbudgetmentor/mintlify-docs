</Check>

> ### **(c) Alcoholic Beverages**
>
> No NSF funds may be requested or spent for alcoholic beverages.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (c) Alcoholic Beverages

<Danger>
  **Zero alcohol. Ever. Anywhere.** Conference receptions, dinners with collaborators, fieldwork "supplies" — no NSF funds for alcohol, full stop.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: If you're hosting an NSF-funded conference and there's a reception with a bar, those drinks must be paid from non-federal funds. Itemize the bill so the alcohol portion is clearly separated.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  This includes "ethanol" used as solvent in research **only if it's the drinkable kind** — lab-grade ethanol/methanol/etc. used as a reagent is fine.
</Warning>
