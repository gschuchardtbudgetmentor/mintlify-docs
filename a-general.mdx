## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(iv) Travel — (a) General (Line E on the Proposal Budget)**
>
> When anticipated, travel and its relation to the proposed activities must be specified, itemized, and justified by destination and cost.
>
> Funds may be requested for field work, attendance at meetings and conferences, and other travel associated with the proposed work, including subsistence. To qualify for support, however, **attendance at meetings or conferences must be necessary to accomplish proposal objectives or disseminate research results**.
>
> Travel support for **dependents of key project personnel** may be requested only when the travel is for a duration of **six months or more** either by inclusion in the approved budget or with the prior written approval of the cognizant NSF Grants and Agreements Officer.
>
> Temporary dependent care costs above and beyond regular dependent care that directly result from travel to conferences are allowable costs provided that the conditions established in **2 CFR § 200.475** are met.
>
> Allowance for **air travel** normally will not exceed the cost of **round-trip, economy airfares**.
>
> Persons traveling under NSF awards must travel by **U.S.-Flag Air carriers**, if available.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (iv) Travel — (a) General

<Tip>
  Plain English: All travel goes on **Line E**. Every trip must be itemized — destination, purpose, # of travelers, # of trips, cost breakdown (airfare \+ lodging \+ per diem) — and tied to the project's goals.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **What you can budget for:**

  - Fieldwork
  - Meetings and conferences (only if necessary for project objectives OR disseminating results)
  - Other travel associated with the proposed work
  - Subsistence (per diem) during travel
</Check>

<Warning>
  "I want to go to a conference" isn't a justification. NSF wants to see WHY the trip advances the project — presenting results, recruiting collaborators, accessing a unique facility, etc.
</Warning>

#### Airfare Rules

<Danger>
  - **Economy class only** for round trips (unless a special exception applies, e.g., disability accommodation, no available economy seats — must be documented).
  - **Fly America Act**: must use a U.S.-Flag air carrier if one is available. Even one leg on a foreign carrier when a U.S. carrier was available is a disallowance.
</Danger>

<Note>
  "Code-share" flights operated by foreign carriers but ticketed by U.S. carriers usually satisfy Fly America. Document the ticket number and flight code in your travel records.
</Note>

#### Dependent Travel

<Tip>
  Plain English: You can request travel costs for dependents (spouse, kids) of key personnel **only if the trip lasts 6 months or more**. Conference-length dependent travel is not budgetable.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Shorter trips can still be approved with prior written approval from the NSF Grants & Agreements Officer — but plan for the default rule.
</Note>

#### Dependent Care During Conference Travel

<Check>
  **Temporary dependent care** costs above your normal childcare costs, incurred specifically because of conference travel, ARE allowable (2 CFR § 200.475). This is the "childcare during a conference" rule — it's a real, modern allowance.
</Check>

#### Itemize, itemize, itemize

<Warning>
  Every trip in the justification needs:

  - Destination
  - Number of travelers
  - Number of trips
  - Purpose (tied to project goals)
  - Cost breakdown: airfare \+ lodging \+ per diem \+ ground transport \+ registration
</Warning>
