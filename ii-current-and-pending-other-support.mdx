## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(ii) Current and Pending (Other) Support**
>
> Current and pending (other) support information is used to assess the capacity or any conflicts of commitment that may impact the ability of the individual to carry out the research effort as proposed. The information also helps assess any potential scientific and budgetary overlap/duplication.
>
> A separate submission must be provided for each proposal and active project, as well as in-kind contributions, using the format specified. Current and pending (other) support must be prepared in SciENcv, which produces an NSF-compliant PDF.
>
> **Consulting activities must be disclosed** under the proposals and active projects section when:
>
> - The consulting requires the senior/key person to perform research;
> - The consulting is related to their research portfolio and could impact funding, time/effort commitments, or scientific integrity; or
> - The consulting contract requires concealing financial/other ties.
>
> Senior/key personnel must disclose contracts associated with foreign-government-sponsored programs (NSPM-33), including foreign talent recruitment programs. Individuals are prohibited from being a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program (42 U.S.C. § 19232).
>
> Do not submit personal information (home address, marital status, hobbies, etc.).
>
> **Format — two types of support:**
>
> **(a) Proposals and Active Projects**
>
> For each: Title, Status (Current/Pending), Proposal/Award Number, Source of Support, Primary Place of Performance, Start/End Date (MM/YYYY), Total Anticipated Amount, Person-Months per year, Overall Objectives (1500 char), Statement of Potential Overlap (enter "none" if none).
>
> **(b) In-Kind Contributions**
>
> Disclose ALL in-kind contributions valued at \$5,000 or more that require a time commitment. For each: Status, Receipt Date, Source, Summary, Person-Months, US Dollar Value, Overall Objectives, Statement of Potential Overlap.
>
> **Certification** — Each senior/key person certifies:
>
> - "I certify that the information provided is current, accurate, and complete…"
> - "I also certify that, at the time of submission, I am not a party in a malign foreign talent recruitment program."

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (ii) Current and Pending (Other) Support

<Tip>
  Plain English: NSF wants to know everything you're already working on (and applying for) so they can check (1) whether you have time to actually do this project and (2) whether there's overlapping scope or budget with other awards.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  Like the Biosketch, Current and Pending **must be generated through SciENcv** — no DIY PDFs allowed. Generate it, certify it inside SciENcv, then upload the SciENcv PDF.
</Danger>

#### What counts as "current" vs "pending"?

<Check>
  - **Current** — any active project or ongoing obligation, regardless of funding source or whether the money flows through your org or directly to you.
  - **Pending** — any proposal (including the one you're submitting!) being considered by any funder.
</Check>

<Warning>
  Yes — you list **this proposal** under Pending. That's not a trick question; NSF expects it.
</Warning>

#### Consulting Disclosure

<Danger>
  You must disclose consulting if any of these are true:

  1. The consulting involves doing research
  2. The consulting relates to your research portfolio and could affect time, funding, or scientific integrity
  3. The consulting contract has a confidentiality clause that hides financial ties

  Plain English: if it's research-adjacent or your contract makes you hide it — disclose it.
</Danger>

#### Foreign Activities

<Warning>
  Disclose all foreign government-sponsored support and any participation in foreign talent recruitment programs. Malign foreign talent recruitment program participation is **prohibited** (42 U.S.C. § 19232).
</Warning>

#### (a) Proposals and Active Projects

<Tip>
  Plain English: For every active and pending project, fill out title, status, award/proposal number, funder, location, dates, total dollar amount, person-months/year, objectives (1500 character limit), and a statement of any overlap with this proposal.
</Tip>

<Note>
  In the "Statement of Potential Overlap" field, if there's no overlap, literally type **"none"**. Don't leave it blank.
</Note>

#### (b) In-Kind Contributions

<Tip>
  Plain English: If anyone outside your org is giving you something valuable (lab space, equipment, data, supplies, student labor) worth **\$5,000\+** AND it eats your time — disclose it.
</Tip>

<Note>
  In-kind under \$5,000 OR with no associated time commitment doesn't need to be reported. Convert foreign currencies to USD at time of submission.
</Note>

#### Certification

<Danger>
  Each senior/key person individually signs inside SciENcv certifying the info is accurate and that they are not party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program. False certifications can lead to criminal prosecution (18 U.S.C. §§ 287, 1001, 1031; 31 U.S.C. §§ 3729-3733, 3802).
</Danger>

<Warning>
  Current and Pending must be **updated prior to award** and at any time NSF determines appropriate during the award. Keep it current — don't just submit-and-forget.
</Warning>

> ### **(ii) Current and Pending (Other) Support**
>
> Current and pending (other) support information is used to assess the capacity or any conflicts of commitment that may impact the ability of the individual to carry out the research effort as proposed. The information also helps assess any potential scientific and budgetary overlap/duplication with the project being proposed.
>
> A separate submission must be provided for each proposal and active project, as well as in-kind contributions. There is no page limitation for this section of the proposal, though some fields have character limitations for consistency and equity.
>
> Current and pending (other) support information must be provided separately for each individual designated as a senior/key person through use of SciENcv (Science Experts Network Curriculum Vitae). SciENcv will produce an NSF-compliant PDF version of current and pending (other) support.
>
> Consulting activities must be disclosed under the proposals and active projects section when:
>
> - The consulting activity requires the senior/key person to perform research as part of the consulting activity;
> - The consulting activity does not involve performing research, but is related to the senior/key person's research portfolio and may have the ability to impact funding, alter time or effort commitments, or otherwise impact scientific integrity; or
> - The consulting entity has provided a contract that requires the senior/key person to conceal or withhold confidential financial or other ties between the senior/key person and the entity.
>
> Consistent with NSPM-33, senior/key personnel are required to disclose contracts associated with participation in programs sponsored by foreign governments, instrumentalities, or entities, including foreign government-sponsored talent recruitment programs. In accordance with 42 U.S.C. § 19232, individuals are prohibited from being a party in a malign foreign talent recruitment program.
>
> Individuals are reminded not to submit any personal information.
>
> The two types of support that must be disclosed are:
>
> **(a) Proposals and Active Projects** — including title, status (Current/Pending), proposal/award number, source of support, primary place of performance, start/end dates, total amount, person-months per year devoted, overall objectives, and a statement of potential overlap.
>
> **(b) In-Kind Contributions** — for any in-kind contribution with an estimated dollar value of \$5,000 or more that requires a commitment of the individual's time. Must include status, receipt date, source, summary, person-months per year, US dollar value, overall objectives, and statement of potential overlap.
>
> Each senior/key person certifies that the information provided is current, accurate, and complete, and that they are not a party in a malign foreign talent recruitment program.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (ii) Current and Pending (Other) Support

Current and pending (other) support information is used to assess the capacity or any conflicts of commitment that may impact the ability of the individual to carry out the research effort as proposed.

<Tip>
  Plain English: NSF uses this to answer two questions: (1) Do you actually have time to do this project, given everything else you've committed to? (2) Are you trying to get paid twice for the same work?
</Tip>

A separate submission must be provided for each proposal and active project, as well as in-kind contributions.

<Tip>
  Plain English: List **every** active grant, **every** pending proposal (including this one), and any qualifying in-kind contributions — regardless of source (federal, state, private foundation, foreign, internal), regardless of whether the money flows through your institution or directly to you.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  SciENcv is required for senior/key personnel. Just like the biosketch — you can't upload a freeform Word document.
</Warning>

<Danger>
  **Consulting must be disclosed** as a Current/Pending project when any of these apply:

  1. The consulting requires you to do research,
  2. The consulting is related to your research portfolio and could affect funding/time/integrity, or
  3. The consulting contract has a confidentiality clause that would hide financial/other ties.

  Many investigators have gotten into serious trouble for treating consulting as casual side income that doesn't need disclosure.
</Danger>

#### (a) Proposals and Active Projects

<Check>
  For each project, you must provide:

  - Title
  - Status (Current or Pending — pending includes this proposal)
  - Proposal/award number (if known)
  - Source of support
  - Primary place of performance
  - Start and end dates
  - Total anticipated amount (entire period, including indirects)
  - Person-months per year you'll devote
  - Overall objectives (1500 character limit)
  - **Statement of potential overlap** (or "none")
</Check>

#### (b) In-Kind Contributions

<Tip>
  Plain English: If someone is giving you \*\*anything worth $5,000+** that requires your time — lab space, equipment, data, samples, a graduate student's effort — you have to disclose it. Below $5,000 or no time commitment? Skip it.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  This is one of the most-overlooked parts of NSPM-33. Foreign in-kind support (data sharing, lab access, sample shipments) almost always triggers disclosure.
</Warning>

<Danger>
  Each senior/key person personally certifies under penalty of perjury that the C&P information is current, accurate, and complete — and that they are not in a malign foreign talent recruitment program. False statements carry criminal exposure under 18 U.S.C. §§ 287, 1001, 1031 and civil exposure under 31 U.S.C. §§ 3729–3733 and 3802.
</Danger>

<Note>
  No personal information (home address, marital status, hobbies, etc.). And C&P disclosures must be refreshed prior to award and any time NSF asks during the award.
</Note>
