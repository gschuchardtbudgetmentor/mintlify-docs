> ### **(vii) Total Direct Costs (Line H on the Proposal Budget)**
>
> The total amount of direct costs requested in the budget, to include Lines A through G, must be entered on Line H.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (vii) Total Direct Costs

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line H is just the sum of Lines A through G. It's the total of every direct cost you're requesting before indirect costs are applied.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Line H = A \+ B \+ C \+ D \+ E \+ F \+ G**

  Where:

  - A = Senior/Key Personnel salaries
  - B = Other Personnel salaries
  - C = Fringe Benefits
  - D = Equipment
  - E = Travel
  - F = Participant Support
  - G = Other Direct Costs (G.1 - G.6)
</Check>

<Note>
  Research.gov calculates Line H automatically. But always double-check the math in your budget justification — typos and rounding errors happen.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Line H is NOT the same as the MTDC base (used to compute indirect). Several Line H items are excluded from MTDC: Equipment, tuition, participant support, and subawards beyond \$25K. See the Indirect Costs page.
</Warning>
