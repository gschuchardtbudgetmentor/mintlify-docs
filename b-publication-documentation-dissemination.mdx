## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(b) Publication/Documentation/Dissemination (Line G.2)**
>
> The proposal budget may request funds for the costs of documenting, preparing, publishing or otherwise making available to others the findings and products of the work to be conducted under the award.
>
> This generally includes the following types of activities:
>
> - reports, reprints, page charges or other journal costs (except costs for prior or early publication);
> - necessary illustrations;
> - cleanup, documentation, storage and indexing of data and databases;
> - development, documentation and debugging of software;
> - and storage, preservation, documentation, indexing, etc., of physical specimens, collections, or fabricated items.
>
> Line G.2 of the proposal budget also may be used to request funding for **data deposit and data curation costs**.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Publication / Documentation / Dissemination

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line G.2 covers everything around getting your results OUT — journal publication fees, illustration costs, data curation/deposit, software documentation, and preservation of specimens or fabricated items.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Common G.2 items:**

  - Open-access article processing charges (APCs)
  - Page charges and color figure fees
  - Reprints
  - Costs of illustrators / graphic designers for figures
  - Data repository deposit fees (Dryad, Zenodo, ICPSR, etc.)
  - Data curation services
  - Software documentation and debugging time
  - Specimen storage and indexing
</Check>

<Warning>
  You cannot charge publication costs for papers **already published** before the award (or from prior unrelated work). Only publications resulting from the project's funded work belong here.
</Warning>

#### Open Access / APCs

<Tip>
  Plain English: Open-access fees are explicitly allowed on G.2. With NSF (and OSTP) increasingly emphasizing public access to research, budgeting realistic APCs is smart.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Typical APCs range from \$1,500 (society journals) to \$10,000\+ (Nature family). Budget by the journals you realistically target.
</Note>

#### Data Management Plan Tie-In

<Danger>
  Your Data Management and Sharing Plan should mention any repository deposit fees you've budgeted on G.2. Reviewers cross-check that the DMSP describes preservation/access that aligns with the budgeted curation costs.
</Danger>

<Check>
  G.2 is included in the **MTDC base** — it generates indirect costs.
</Check>
