## 1. Create Your Account

To get started, visit:

[https://budgetmentor.com](https://budgetmentor.com)

Click **Sign Up** and create an account using your email or Google login.

Once your account is created, you will be asked to create or join a workspace.

---

## 2. Create a Workspace

A workspace is where your organization, lab, department, or consulting team manages budgets.

Example workspace names:

| Workspace Type | Example Name |
| :-- | :-- |
| University department | Biology Department |
| Research lab | Smith Lab |
| Consultant team | Grants Advisory Group |
| Company | Acme Research LLC |

To create a workspace:

1. Click **Create Workspace**
2. Enter your workspace name
3. Add your organization name
4. Choose your role
5. Click **Continue**

---

## 3. Create Your First Budget

After creating a workspace, click **New Budget**.

You may be asked to enter:

- Project title
- Sponsor
- Program type
- Budget duration
- Start date
- End date
- Principal Investigator
- Organization

Example:

```text
```

```text
Project Title: AI-Assisted NSF Budget Development
Sponsor: National Science Foundation
Budget Period: 3 years
Start Date: 09/01/2026
End Date: 08/31/2029
Principal Investigator: Dr. Jane Smith
Organization: Example University
```

---

## 4. Add Senior Personnel

Use the **Personnel** section to add the Principal Investigator, Co-PIs, faculty, researchers, and other key team members.

Budget Mentor supports common NSF-style personnel fields, including:

| Field | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| Name | Person’s full name |
| Role | PI, Co-PI, Senior Personnel, Researcher, etc. |
| Calendar Months | Total calendar-month effort |
| Academic Months | Academic-year effort |
| Summer Months | Summer effort |
| Base Salary | Institutional base salary |
| Fringe Rate | Applicable fringe benefit rate |

Example personnel entry:

```text
```

```text
{
  "name": "Dr. Jane Smith",
  "role": "Principal Investigator",
  "calendarMonths": 1.2,
  "academicMonths": 0,
  "summerMonths": 1.2,
  "baseSalary": 120000,
  "fringeRate": 0.32
}
```

## 5. Add Budget Categories

Budget Mentor helps organize common NSF budget categories.

Typical categories include:

- Senior Personnel
- Other Personnel
- Fringe Benefits
- Equipment
- Travel
- Participant Support Costs
- Materials and Supplies
- Consultant Services
- Computer Services
- Subawards
- Other Direct Costs
- Indirect Costs\

> **Tip:** If you are unsure where a cost belongs, add a note or comment for your sponsored programs office to review later.

---

## 6. Invite Collaborators

You can invite team members to view, comment on, or edit a budget.

Budget Mentor supports multiple roles:

| Role | Best For | Permissions |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Viewer | Department leadership, reviewers | View and export |
| Commenter | Advisors, reviewers | View and comment |
| Planner | Early-stage collaborators | Use planning tools |
| Collaborator | Budget contributors | Edit budget inputs |
| Owner | Admins and project leads | Manage budget, members, and billing |

To invite a collaborator:

1. \
   Open the budget\
2. \
   Click **Share**\
3. \
   Enter the collaborator’s email\
4. \
   Choose a role\
5. \
   Click **Send Invite**\

---

## 7. Use Comments and Notes

Use comments to ask questions, flag issues, or explain assumptions.

Example comments:

> “Please confirm whether this consultant rate is allowed under the solicitation.”

> “Can we verify whether this equipment item should be excluded from indirect cost calculations?”

> “This travel estimate assumes two domestic conferences per year.”

## 8. Use Budget Mentor AI

Budget Mentor AI can help explain budget categories, flag missing information, and help draft budget justifications.

Example questions you can ask:

```text
```

```text
Does this NSF budget appear to be missing any common categories?

Please help me draft a short justification for domestic travel.

Explain the difference between participant support costs and other direct costs.

What should I check before exporting this to NSF format?
```

> **Important:** AI-generated answers should be reviewed by a human. Budget Mentor AI is designed to assist, not replace, institutional review.

## 14. Common Issues

### I invited someone, but they cannot access the budget

Check that:

- \
  The invite was sent to the correct email\
- \
  The collaborator accepted the invitation\
- \
  They are logged into the correct account\
- \
  Their workspace role allows access\
- \
  The budget was shared with them directly or through the workspace\

---

### My totals do not look right

Review:

- \
  Salary base\
- \
  Effort months\
- \
  Fringe rate\
- \
  Indirect cost rate\
- \
  Exclusions from the indirect cost base\
- \
  Equipment classification\
- \
  Participant support cost treatment\
- \
  Subaward indirect cost rules\

---

### I am not sure where to put a cost

Use comments or AI assistance to flag the issue. Then confirm with your institution’s sponsored programs office.

Example:

```text
```

```text
I am not sure whether this item belongs under Materials and Supplies or Equipment. Please review before submission.
```

## 16. FAQ

### Does Budget Mentor submit my proposal to NSF?

No. Budget Mentor helps prepare and organize budget information. You should still follow your institution’s official submission process.

### Can I collaborate with my team?

Yes. You can invite collaborators with different roles, including Viewer, Commenter, Planner, Collaborator, and Owner.

### Can Budget Mentor help with budget justifications?

Yes. Budget Mentor AI can help draft and review budget justification language, but all AI-generated text should be reviewed before submission.

### Can I export my budget?

Yes. Budget Mentor is designed to help users export budget information for review, sharing, and sponsor preparation.

### Is Budget Mentor a replacement for my sponsored programs office?

No. Budget Mentor is a drafting and collaboration tool. Your sponsored programs office should still review your proposal before submission.
