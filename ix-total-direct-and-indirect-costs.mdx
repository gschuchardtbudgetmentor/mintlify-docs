## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(ix) Total Direct and Indirect Costs (F&A) (Line J on the Proposal Budget)**
>
> The total amount of direct and indirect costs (F&A) (sum of Lines H and I) must be entered on line J.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (ix) Total Direct and Indirect Costs

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line J = Line H \+ Line I. Direct costs plus indirect costs. This is the total cost of the project — before fees (Line K).
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Line J = Line H (Total Direct) \+ Line I (Indirect)**
</Check>

<Note>
  Research.gov auto-calculates this. Double-check the math, especially after changes — a wrong F&A calc cascades into Line J.
</Note>

<Warning>
  For most non-SBIR/STTR/Major Facilities proposals, Line J is the same as Line L (Amount of This Request) since there are no fees.
</Warning>
