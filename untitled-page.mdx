> ### **(d) Home Office Workspace**
>
> Rental of any property owned by individuals or entities affiliated with NSF recipients (including commercial or residential real estate), for use as home office workspace is unallowable. See 2 CFR § 200.465(f).

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (d) Home Office Workspace

<Danger>
  You **cannot** use NSF funds to rent any property — residential or commercial — that is owned by individuals or entities **affiliated with the NSF recipient** for use as a home office workspace.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: A PI (or their spouse, their LLC, or any affiliated entity) cannot rent out part of their own house to the project as a home office and charge it to NSF.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  The rule covers any property owned by an "affiliated" individual or entity. This blocks self-dealing arrangements where institutions or staff effectively pay themselves rent through the grant.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Off-campus research or field workspace leased from a **third party unrelated** to the recipient may still be allowable if reasonable and properly justified — that's a different situation.
</Note>
