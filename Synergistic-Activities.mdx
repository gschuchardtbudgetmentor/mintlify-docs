## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(iv) Synergistic Activities**
>
> Each individual identified as a senior/key person must provide a document of up to one page that includes a list of up to five distinct examples that demonstrate the broader impact of the individual's professional and scholarly activities that focus on the integration and transfer of knowledge as well as its creation.
>
> Senior/key personnel must prepare, save, and submit these documents as part of their proposal via Research.gov or Grants.gov.
>
> Examples may include, among others:
>
> - innovations in teaching and training;
> - contributions to the science of learning;
> - development and/or refinement of research tools;
> - computation methodologies and algorithms for problem-solving;
> - development of databases to support research and education;
> - broadening the participation of groups underrepresented in STEM;
> - participation in international research collaborations;
> - participation in national and/or international standards development efforts; and
> - service to the scientific and engineering community outside of the individual's immediate organization.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (iv) Synergistic Activities

<Tip>
  Plain English: One-pager from each senior/key person showing **broader impact** activities outside of pure research — teaching innovations, outreach, tool/database development, standards committees, mentoring underrepresented groups, etc.
</Tip>

<Check>
  - **One page maximum** per senior/key person.
  - **Up to 5 distinct examples** — pick your best 5, not all of them.
  - Each senior/key person submits their own.
</Check>

<Warning>
  Reviewers explicitly evaluate broader impacts — synergistic activities is one of the few places you get to show this off outside of the Project Description. Don't phone it in.
</Warning>

#### Examples NSF suggests:

<Info>
  - Innovations in teaching and training
  - Contributions to the science of learning
  - Development/refinement of research tools, methodologies, algorithms
  - Development of databases to support research and education
  - Broadening participation of underrepresented groups in STEM
  - International research collaborations
  - National/international standards development
  - Service to the scientific community outside your own organization
</Info>

<Tip>
  Plain English: "Distinct" means 5 _different kinds_ of things, not 5 variations of the same activity. Mix teaching \+ outreach \+ tool-building \+ service to show range.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Submit through Research.gov or Grants.gov as part of the proposal — not a single-copy doc.
</Note>
