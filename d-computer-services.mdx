## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(d) Computer Services (Line G.4)**
>
> The cost of computer services, including computer-based retrieval of scientific, technical, and educational information, may be requested only where it is **institutional policy to charge such costs as direct charges**.
>
> A justification based on the established computer service rates at the proposing organization must be included.
>
> The proposal budget also may request costs for **leasing of computer equipment**.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (d) Computer Services

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line G.4 is for computing services you're being charged for by an internal core facility or external provider — HPC time, cloud computing (AWS / Azure / GCP), specialized databases, etc.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Examples of G.4 items:**

  - University HPC center hours (at established recharge rates)
  - Cloud computing (AWS, GCP, Azure)
  - Scientific database subscriptions (e.g., specialized data services)
  - Computer equipment **leasing** costs
</Check>

<Danger>
  G.4 only works if your **institutional policy** is to charge these as direct costs. If your institution treats computing as indirect, you can't direct-charge it. Confirm with SPO before budgeting.
</Danger>

#### Justification Required

<Warning>
  Your budget justification must reference your institution's **established computer service rates**. "Estimated \$5,000 for cloud" isn't enough — show the rate (e.g., "\$0.10/CPU-hour × 50,000 hours = \$5,000") and tie it to your computational plan.
</Warning>

#### Leasing vs Purchase

<Tip>
  Plain English: Leasing a computer or HPC node goes on G.4. **Purchasing** a computer of \$5,000\+ goes on Line D (Equipment); under \$5,000 goes on G.1 (Supplies).
</Tip>

<Note>
  Cloud computing is the most common modern G.4 expense. Budget with realistic usage estimates and consider that prices and discounts change year to year.
</Note>

<Check>
  G.4 is included in the **MTDC base** — it generates indirect costs.
</Check>
