## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

<Note>
  Skip to Part 2 for an annotated human readable version of this raw NSF Guidelines text.
</Note>

> ### **(viii) Indirect Costs / F&A (Line I on the Proposal Budget)**
>
> Except where specifically identified in an NSF program solicitation, the applicable **U.S. Federally negotiated indirect cost rate(s) must be used** in computing indirect costs (F&A) for a proposal.
>
> Use of an indirect cost rate lower than the organization's current negotiated indirect cost rate is considered a **violation of NSF's cost sharing policy**.
>
> The amount for indirect costs must be calculated by applying the current negotiated indirect cost rate(s) to the approved base(s), and such amounts must be specified in the budget justification.
>
> Indirect cost recovery for IHEs is additionally restricted by 2 CFR § 200, Appendix III, paragraph C.7., which specifies Federal agencies are required to use the negotiated F&A rate that is in effect at the time of the initial award **throughout the life of the sponsored agreement**.
>
> **Organizations without a current NICRA** may choose to apply the **de minimis rate of 10% to a base of modified total direct costs (MTDC)** as authorized by 2 CFR § 200.414(f). No supporting documentation is required for proposed rates of 10% or less of MTDC.
>
> Organizations without a current NICRA wishing to request indirect rate recovery above 10% should prepare an indirect cost proposal based on expenditures for its most recently ended fiscal year.
>
> **NSF does not negotiate rates for entities that do not yet hold direct NSF funding, nor does NSF negotiate rates for subrecipients.**
>
> **Foreign organizations** that do not have a current U.S. Federally negotiated indirect cost rate are limited to the de minimis 10% of MTDC. Foreign recipients that have a U.S. negotiated rate may recover at the current negotiated rate.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (viii) Indirect Costs (F&A)

<Tip>
  Plain English: Line I = indirect costs (also called Facilities & Administrative costs, or F&A). These are the costs of running your institution that can't be tied to one specific project — building maintenance, utilities, departmental admin, library, IT infrastructure, etc.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  **You must use your full federally-negotiated F&A rate.** Voluntarily using a lower rate (e.g., "we'll only charge 30% to look attractive") = NSF cost sharing violation. Don't do it.
</Danger>

#### The Formula

<Check>
  **Indirect Costs = Negotiated F&A Rate × Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC)**

  MTDC = Total Direct Costs (Line H) **MINUS**:

  - Equipment (Line D)
  - Tuition remission
  - Rental costs of off-site facilities
  - Scholarships and fellowships
  - Participant Support (Line F)
  - Portions of each subaward exceeding \$25,000
</Check>

#### Multiple Rates

<Warning>
  Most institutions have multiple F&A rates:

  - **On-campus research** rate (highest, ~50-70%)
  - **Off-campus research** rate (lower)
  - **Instruction** rate
  - **Other sponsored activities** rate

  Use the correct one. Most NSF awards use the on-campus research rate. If your project is field-based or based at a remote site, off-campus may apply.
</Warning>

#### Rate Lock for IHEs (the big rule)

<Danger>
  For Institutions of Higher Education: per 2 CFR § 200 Appendix III, paragraph C.7, the **F&A rate in effect at the time of award is locked in for the entire life of that award**. Don't escalate F&A rates across budget years for IHEs.
</Danger>

#### No NICRA? You Have Two Options

<Tip>
  Plain English: If your institution doesn't have a current federally-negotiated rate:

  1. **Use 10% de minimis** of MTDC — no documentation needed
  2. **Propose a higher rate** by preparing an indirect cost proposal based on your most recent fiscal year expenditures — NSF may negotiate an award-specific rate or issue a full NICRA
</Tip>

<Warning>
  NSF won't negotiate rates for entities that don't already hold direct NSF funding, nor for subrecipients. Subrecipients use their own rate or 10% de minimis (see the Subawards page).
</Warning>

#### Foreign Organizations

<Note>
  Foreign organizations without a US-federally-negotiated rate are capped at **10% de minimis of MTDC**. Foreign orgs with a negotiated US rate can use that rate.
</Note>

#### Budget Justification

<Check>
  The budget justification must show:

  - Which F&A rate(s) you're using
  - The MTDC base calculation (which Line items you included/excluded)
  - The indirect cost calculation (rate × base = Line I)
</Check>
