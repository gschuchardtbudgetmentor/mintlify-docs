## Information Key

## **<u>Part 1: Text Quote Directly from PAPPG (NSF 24-1)</u>**

<Check>
  NSF 24-1 applies to all proposals submitted or due on or after May 20, 2024.
</Check>

> ### **(b) Meals and Coffee Breaks**
>
> No funds may be requested for meals or coffee breaks for intramural meetings of an organization or any of its components, including, but not limited to, laboratories, departments, and centers. (See 2 CFR § 200.432 for additional information on the charging of certain types of costs generally associated with conferences supported by NSF.) Meal expenses of recipient employees who are not on travel status are unallowable. See also 2 CFR § 200.438.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Meals and Coffee Breaks

<Danger>
  **No meals or coffee breaks** for any intramural meetings — lab meetings, department meetings, center meetings, retreats on your own campus. Unallowable.
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: You can't budget pizza for a lab meeting, donuts for a brainstorming session, or catering for a department-only event.
</Tip>

#### Exceptions

<Check>
  Allowable meal costs:

  - NSF-sponsored **conferences** open to outside participants (per 2 CFR § 200.432)
  - Meals when employees are actually **on travel status**
  - Participant support meals at qualifying training events
</Check>

<Warning>
  "On travel status" matters. Bringing a guest collaborator to your campus for a meeting doesn't make your team "on travel status" — those meals are unallowable.
</Warning>

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (b) Meals and Coffee Breaks

<Danger>
  **Unallowable for internal meetings.** No NSF funds for meals or coffee breaks at intramural meetings — meaning meetings within your own organization (lab meetings, department meetings, center retreats, etc.).
</Danger>

<Tip>
  Plain English: Buying donuts and coffee for your weekly lab meeting? Not on NSF's dime. The same goes for catered department lunches and similar internal events.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Meals during legitimate travel (per diem on official travel status) are allowable under your travel policy. The restriction is for meetings **at your home institution**, not for travel-status meals.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Conferences and workshops that are an explicit project deliverable can have meals — but those are typically budgeted under Participant Support (Line F) or Other Direct Costs (Line G.6).
</Note>
