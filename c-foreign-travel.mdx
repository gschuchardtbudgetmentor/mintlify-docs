> ### **(c) Foreign Travel**
>
> Travel outside the areas specified above is considered foreign travel.
>
> When anticipated, the proposer must enter the names of **countries** and **dates of visit** on the proposal budget, if known.

## **<u>Part 2: PAPPG Translated Into Plain English</u>**

### (c) Foreign Travel

<Tip>
  Plain English: Anything outside the U.S., its territories, and possessions = foreign travel. Even Canada or Mexico is "foreign" for NSF purposes.
</Tip>

<Check>
  For each foreign trip, on the proposal budget you must enter (if known):

  - **Country/countries** to be visited
  - **Dates** of visit
</Check>

<Warning>
  This is a hard NSF requirement — if you have foreign travel and don't list the country, the budget is incomplete. Even if exact dates aren't known yet, give your best estimate ("Year 2, summer").
</Warning>

#### Cover Sheet Implications

<Danger>
  Foreign travel often triggers the **International Activities** box on the Cover Sheet. You must also list the country/countries in the **International Activities Country Name(s)** field. See the Cover Sheet page for details.
</Danger>

#### Allowability Rules (same as domestic, plus)

<Tip>
  Plain English: Foreign travel still has to be:

  - Economy class
  - On a U.S.-Flag carrier when available (Fly America)
  - Necessary for project objectives
  - Itemized and justified

  Plus: per diem rates come from the **U.S. Department of State** Foreign Per Diem Rates (not GSA).
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Open Skies Agreements (with EU, Switzerland, Japan, Australia) can let you fly on certain foreign carriers and still comply with Fly America. Check the current State Department list before booking.
</Warning>

#### Conference Travel Abroad

<Note>
  International conferences must be justified the same way domestic ones are — explicit project-objective benefit. Just "the conference is in my field" isn't enough.
</Note>

#### Documentation in Budget Justification

<Check>
  Each foreign trip should detail:

  - Traveler(s) and role
  - Country/destination city
  - Dates / duration
  - Purpose (project-objective tie-in)
  - Cost breakdown: airfare \+ lodging \+ DoS per diem \+ registration \+ ground transport
</Check>
